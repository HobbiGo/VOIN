FAQ

How do I buy VOIN tokens?

What is the minimum and maximum purchase per batch?

Minimum purchase is $10, and maximum purchase per batch is unlimited as long as it does not exceed the remaining 15,600,000 VOIN quota in the active batch.

When does the presale run?

The official presale schedule runs from December 2026 to December 2028.
